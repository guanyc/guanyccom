This hub brings together public-facing articles for the `KJV` app line.

The main focus is practical guidance around:

- offline KJV Bible reading
- stronger Bible verse search
- daily devotional rhythm
- guided reading plans
- emotion-based entry through `Take a Break`
- evening verse and sleep audio support

The larger idea behind these pages is simple: the KJV Bible made for the real moments of a normal day.
