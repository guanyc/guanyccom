When you feel anxious, one of the hardest parts is knowing where to begin.

You may want to read Scripture.

But if your mind is already racing, even choosing a passage can feel like too much.

That is why it often helps to start with a small group of **Bible verses for anxiety** that are easier to receive in that kind of moment.

Not every passage meets anxiety in the same way.

Some verses steady the mind.
Some remind you that God is near.
Some help you release what you have been carrying.

Here are a few **KJV Bible verses** that many people turn to when they feel anxious.

## 1. Philippians 4:6-7

> Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God.
> And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

This is one of the clearest passages for anxious thoughts.

It does not pretend worry is not real.

It gives you a direction for it:

- bring it to God
- pray honestly
- let peace guard your heart and mind

This verse is often helpful when anxiety feels mental and restless, especially when your thoughts keep circling.

## 2. Isaiah 41:10

> Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

This verse is strong without being harsh.

It does not only say "do not fear."

It also says why:

- God is with you
- God will help you
- God will hold you up

For many people, this passage is helpful when anxiety feels tied to uncertainty, pressure, or the fear of not being able to carry what is ahead.

## 3. Psalm 56:3

> What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.

This is a short verse, but that is part of its strength.

Sometimes a shorter line is easier to hold onto than a longer passage.

It gives you something simple enough to repeat when your mind feels crowded.

This verse is especially useful when you need a small anchor rather than a long reading session.

## 4. Matthew 11:28-30

> Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.
> Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls.
> For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.

Anxiety often comes with heaviness.

This passage meets that heaviness with invitation, not pressure.

Jesus does not begin by adding another demand.

He begins with rest.

That makes this a good passage when anxiety is mixed with exhaustion, emotional overload, or the feeling that you cannot keep carrying everything the same way.

## 5. John 14:27

> Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.

This verse is especially helpful when you need reassurance more than explanation.

It centers peace as something given, not something you manufacture.

That matters, because anxious people are often already trying very hard to calm themselves down.

This verse gently shifts the focus from self-management to Christ's peace.

## What kind of Bible verses for anxiety help most?

Different passages help in different ways.

For example:

- **Philippians 4:6-7** helps when you need peace over racing thoughts
- **Isaiah 41:10** helps when you need strength and reassurance
- **Psalm 56:3** helps when you need a short repeatable anchor
- **Matthew 11:28-30** helps when anxiety feels like emotional exhaustion
- **John 14:27** helps when you need calm and Christ-centered peace

The goal is not to read a large amount all at once.

The goal is to begin somewhere that your heart can actually receive.
