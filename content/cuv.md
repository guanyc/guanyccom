Chinese Bible (CUV) is a lightweight Bible and devotional app built around the **Chinese Union Version (和合本)**.

It’s designed for everyday moments—when you want Scripture that’s easy to access, simple to understand, and gentle to return to.

## A long-running Chinese Bible app

This Chinese Union Version Bible app has been available since **2010** and has continued serving users for **15+ years**.

That matters because many people are not only looking for Bible features. They also want something steady, familiar, and trustworthy enough to keep using over time.

For a Bible app, longevity itself is part of the value:

- it shows long-term care rather than a short-lived release
- it reflects years of updates across changing Android devices and reading habits
- it suggests a product people can return to, not just try once
